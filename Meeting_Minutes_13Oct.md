## Purpose
Discuss the TutorLink Phase 2 Software Requirements Specification (SRS), team responsibilities, project-management evidence, and submission preparation.

## Main Discussion Points
1. TutorLink scope and the three main roles: Seeker, Tutor, and Coordinator.
2. Booking rules:
   - No overlapping confirmed tutor sessions.
   - Tutor must be verified for the requested subject.
   - Maximum 5 tutoring hours in a rolling 7-day period without coordinator approval.
   - Requests must be submitted at least 2 hours before the proposed start time.
   - Session duration must be 30–120 minutes.
   - Cancellation at least 4 hours before the session is timely.
   - Important actions must be recorded in the audit log.
3. Extension F: mock notifications and reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before a confirmed session.
4. Functional requirements, use cases, non-functional requirements, domain model, and UI prototype.
5. Submission preparation: SRS, repository link, WBS, Gantt chart, and meeting evidence.
